# Legal — memos, drafts and open questions

The General Counsel writes here. One file per question:
`YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`.

Every memo has the same shape, so a reader can check it without trusting it:

1. **Question** — one sentence, in the words of the card.
2. **Facts assumed** — what the product does, with a link to the source
   (`docs/MVP-V0.1.md`, `docs/CLAIMS.md`, a card, a runbook).
3. **Jurisdiction** — governing law, and why that one.
4. **Sources** — instrument, article/section, version in force, date checked,
   link.
5. **Analysis** — binding law, then guidance, then the gap between them.
6. **Risk** — low / medium / high, with the driver named.
7. **Recommendation** — and, where wording matters, the exact sentence.
8. **What would change the answer** — the facts or instruments to watch.
9. **Open questions for Savio** — entity, contracting party, customer mix.

Closing line, always: *research memo, not legal advice; a licensed lawyer signs
before external reliance.*
